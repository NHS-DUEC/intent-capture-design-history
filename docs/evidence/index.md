This section is used to connect design history claims to supporting evidence.

Evidence may include:

- user research
- analytics
- workshop outputs
- meeting notes
- pilot findings
- technical investigation
- clinical safety input
- product or policy decisions

## Evidence levels

Use the following labels when writing or reviewing content:

### Established

Supported by agreed decisions, research, operational evidence or repeated stakeholder alignment.

### Emerging

Seen across multiple discussions or artefacts, but not yet formally agreed.

### Hypothesis

A proposed idea that still needs to be tested.

### Open question

A material uncertainty that still needs a decision, evidence or further exploration.
