Decision records capture important choices and the reasoning behind them. They are deliberately shorter than design history posts and should be useful when someone asks why the team chose one approach over another.

Each decision record should include:

- the status of the decision
- the context for the decision
- the decision itself
- consequences and trade-offs
- unresolved questions, where relevant
